# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We pledge to interact in ways that contribute to an open and healthy community.

## Our Standards
Examples of behavior that contributes to a healthy community include:
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:
- The use of sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
- Other forms of behavior otherwise deemed harmful, inappropriate, or unwelcome

## Enforcement
Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair steps to actually enforce them.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for doing so.

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, and also applies when an individual is representing the project in public spaces. Examples of contributing project spaces include:
- Mailing lists, IRC, Slack, Discord, and other community chat tools
- Official project websites, forums, and social media accounts
- GitHub issues, pull requests, and discussions
- Project-sponsored events

## Reporting
Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project team responsible for enforcement. Incident reports should be made to nanobus.net@gmail.com. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

All reports will be kept confidential. The project team is obligated to protect the privacy and safety of the reporter of the incident.

## Enforcement Process
Community leaders will follow these steps to address a reported incident:
1. **Acknowledgement:** The reporter will receive a confirmation that the report was received.
2. **Investigation:** The team will review the report and gather evidence.
3. **Resolution:** Depending on the severity, the resolution may include:
    - A private warning.
    - A public apology.
    - Temporary restriction of access to project spaces.
    - Permanent ban from the project and all associated spaces.
